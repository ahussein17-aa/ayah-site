Ayah Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

Ayah collects no data. None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party services. It works entirely offline.

What the app stores

Everything you do in Ayah stays on your device. Your saved signs, recently viewed items, and appearance preference are stored in the app's local storage on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. The app makes no network connections at all.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. There is nothing held anywhere else.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, for example by adding an online feature, this policy will be updated first, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.
