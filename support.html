Content corrections

Every sign in Ayah carries an honesty badge (established, classical, or discussed), so the app never presents a contested reading as settled. If you believe a verse, translation, attribution, or badge is wrong, please email with the sign's title and the issue. Corrections are taken seriously and shipped in updates.

Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection? No. All content is included with the app and everything works offline.

Where are my saved signs? In the Saved tab. They are stored only on your device. See the privacy policy.

What do the badges mean? Established means the verse's plain meaning or a widely accepted reading; classical means a long-standing interpretation from the classical works; discussed means a reading that is genuinely debated, presented as such. The full guide is in the app under More → "How to read these signs".

How do I switch light / dark mode? More → Appearance, or leave it on Auto to follow your device.
